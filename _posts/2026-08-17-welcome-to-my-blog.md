Welcome to my blog. This is where I will share notes, research ideas, and reflections on topics that I am currently exploring.

<!--more-->

## What I plan to write about

My interests span several connected areas:

- robotics and embodied intelligence;
- world models and reinforcement learning;
- mathematics and theoretical machine learning;
- quantitative finance and stochastic processes.

The goal is not only to record finished ideas, but also to make the learning process visible and useful.

## Publishing a new post

To publish another article, copy this file into the `_posts` directory and rename it using:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-post-title.md
```

Update the metadata between the two `---` lines, then write the article below it using Markdown. GitHub Pages will automatically add the article to the blog after the file is pushed.

> Clear notes are a way to test whether an idea has really been understood.

I look forward to documenting that process here.
